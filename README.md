# Download Obsidian B2B

Obsidian B2B lets two DJs play back to back from different homes, passing the booth live over the internet.

These are early test builds. No account is needed to download.

## Windows 10 or 11

**[Download for Windows](https://github.com/briankaspiev-bit/obsidian-b2b-downloads/releases/download/windows-build/Obsidian-windows-setup.exe)**

Windows will say it doesn't recognize the app. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. Full steps: [install-windows.md](install-windows.md)

## Mac (Apple chip or Intel, macOS 11 or newer)

**[Download for Mac](https://github.com/briankaspiev-bit/obsidian-b2b-downloads/releases/download/mac-build/Obsidian-mac.dmg)**

The first time you open it, go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**, then allow the microphone and network. Full steps: [install-mac.md](install-mac.md)
